
# MediQueue

# Live site (client-side): https://teacher-khuji.vercel.app

**MediQueue** is a tutor booking website where students can sign up, log in, explore tutors, and book online classes based on subjects and available time slots. After booking a session, the system creates a digital booking token, and students can easily manage their upcoming classes. The main goal of the platform is to make tutor booking simple and organized by reducing manual work, avoiding schedule conflicts, and providing a smooth learning experience for students.


Features:

-Search and explore tutor profiles with easy filtering options.
-Book, reschedule, or cancel tutoring sessions quickly and securely.
-Manage your bookings and view previous session history from your profile.
-Give ratings and reviews to help other students choose the right tutor.
-Mobile-friendly and responsive design built with Next.js for a smooth experience.
-Tutor dashboard where tutors can manage schedules and session requests.
